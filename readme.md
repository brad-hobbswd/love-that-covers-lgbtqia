# Love That Covers LGBTQIA+

A journey through faith, belonging, healing, and the love of Jesus.

Love That Covers LGBTQIA+ is an online book experience by Bradley Hobbs that explores the lives, stories, struggles, hopes, and faith journeys of LGBTQIA+ people through grace, compassion, understanding, and spiritual reflection. Through personal stories, resources, prayer, and honest conversation, readers are invited into a journey toward healing, belonging, hope, and the enduring love of Jesus.

This project was designed as an immersive GitHub Pages reading experience where each section of the book unfolds as its own page, creating a steady reading flow from beginning to conclusion.

## About the Author

Bradley Hobbs writes with the conviction that Jesus still heals wounded hearts and reaches people who have felt unseen, unheard, rejected, or forgotten.

His writing seeks to encourage readers through devotionals, stories, and reflections rooted in faith, hope, healing, grace, compassion, and restoration.
