The heart behind the book

“You are deeply loved. Fully seen. Eternally cherished.”

A story of belonging · healing · grace

Love does not ignore the wound. It covers it with grace.

Love That Covers is a story for those who have carried silence, rejection, and questions, yet still long to know they are fully seen, deeply loved, and never beyond the reach of God’s grace.

Love is not measured by perfection. It is not limited by human judgment. It reaches into the deepest places, restoring what has been broken and holding what others have cast aside.
