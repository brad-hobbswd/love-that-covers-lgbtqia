A work of compassion and belonging

Love That Covers: Stories of dignity, faith, and belonging.

Love That Covers is a story for those who have carried silence, rejection, and questions, yet still long to know they are fully seen, deeply loved, and never beyond the reach of God’s grace.

Through personal stories and thoughtful reflection, this book considers the impact of rejection and the importance of meeting every person with dignity, patience, and care.

Where this edition began

One message of love, a new story to tell.

The first edition of Love That Covers explored the places where hurt, forgiveness, faith, and healing meet. At its heart was a conviction that the love of Jesus can reach people in the places where life has wounded them.

This LGBTQIA+ edition grew from that same starting point. It brings that message into a story shaped around identity, rejection, faith, and the longing to belong. It gives particular attention to people who have felt unseen, misunderstood, or pushed away, and to the importance of meeting every life with dignity and compassion.

This is not a sequel or a replacement for the first book. It is a distinct edition with its own stories and perspective, connected to the original by the belief that no person should be beyond the reach of love.
