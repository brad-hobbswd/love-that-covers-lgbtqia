Opening pages

Preface

Love That Covers began with a conviction that love can meet people in the places where life has left them wounded. The first edition explored hurt, forgiveness, faith, and restoration. This LGBTQIA+ edition grows from that foundation as a distinct story focused on identity, rejection, faith, and the longing to belong without having to hide.

At the center is a quilt assembled through the hands of people with different histories. As it moves from one person to another, it becomes a reminder that every piece matters, every story deserves to be heard, and compassion can connect people who might otherwise feel alone.

The chapters invite readers to consider what people carry, what remains after love has been given or withheld, and how connection can grow when people choose to meet one another with dignity and care.

The invitation

Forward

This story begins with a quilt, but what it carries is much larger than fabric. It carries memory, welcome, grief, courage, and the possibility of being seen.

Across five chapters, the quilt connects people whose experiences are different but whose need for understanding is deeply human. The story asks readers to consider the difference between speaking about love and allowing love to shape the way they listen, welcome, remember, and remain present.

Readers are invited to approach each person with curiosity rather than quick judgment, recognizing that no one piece carries the whole design and no one person creates belonging alone.

May these pages encourage honest reflection, deepen compassion, and remind you that every life is worthy of being met with care.
