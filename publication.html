Where the book began

Love That Covers was first created in 2025, when the original edition came about. Its foundation was a message about hurt, forgiveness, faith, healing, and the kind of love that meets people in difficult places. That first edition established the heart of the work and the quilt as a picture of stories, lives, and experiences woven together.

The LGBTQIA+ edition developed from that beginning as a distinct work. It carries forward the original concern for compassion and restoration while giving particular attention to identity, rejection, faith, and belonging. It is neither a replacement for the first edition nor simply a reprint. It is a separate telling shaped around its own characters, chapters, and perspective.
