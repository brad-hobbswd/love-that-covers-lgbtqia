With compassion and hope

Dedication

This book is dedicated to the LGBTQIA+ community and to every person who has ever carried the weight of rejection, shame, fear, or misunderstanding.

To those who have felt unseen, unheard, or made to believe they must hide a part of their story to be worthy of love, may these pages remind you that your life has dignity, your story matters, and you deserve to be met with compassion.

To every person searching for belonging, to those rebuilding trust after painful experiences, and to the people who choose to stand beside them with patience and kindness, this dedication is for you.

May you find hope in the reminder that no wound makes you less worthy of care, no difficult chapter tells the whole of your story, and love can still reach the places where hurt has lived.

With hope, Bradley Hobbs
