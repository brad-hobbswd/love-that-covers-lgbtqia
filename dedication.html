With compassion and hope

Dedication

I dedicate this book to the LGBTQIA+ community, and especially to those who have spent too many days wondering where they belong.

To the person who has cried in private and then stepped back into the world as though nothing happened. To the one who has searched a room for a familiar face, hoped someone would understand, or held back the truth because the cost of being misunderstood felt too great. I see the weight those moments can carry, and I hope these pages meet you with tenderness.

If I could, I would sit beside every person who has been made to feel different, unwanted, or difficult to love. I would remind you that you should never have had to beg for kindness, and that the painful things spoken over you do not get to define the whole of your story. I cannot change what happened, but I hope this book can offer a quiet reminder that you matter.

To those still searching for a place where they can breathe, where they can speak honestly, and where they do not have to make themselves smaller to be welcomed, I hope you find a little of that peace here. You do not have to have every answer today. You deserve patience while you find your way.

To everyone who has chosen to listen, learn, stay, and love when turning away would have been easier, thank you. Your kindness may seem small to you, but it may become the moment someone remembers when they need a reason to hope.

The quilt in this story is made of many pieces, just as every life holds its own memories, questions, joys, and sorrows. To every person who has wondered whether their piece belongs, I want you to hear this: it does. You do.

If these pages bring tears, I hope they also bring comfort. May you close this book knowing that your story matters, that you deserve to be treated with dignity, and that there is still room for tenderness, connection, and love.

With all my heart, Bradley Hobbs
