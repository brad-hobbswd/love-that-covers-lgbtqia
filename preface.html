Preface

Love That Covers was born from a simple but powerful truth: love has the capacity to reach places that judgment cannot. For anyone who has spent years carrying questions about identity, belonging, faith, or worth, that truth can feel both difficult and deeply necessary.

This LGBTQIA+ edition was written for people who have experienced rejection, silence, shame, misunderstanding, or the painful feeling of being outside the places where they once hoped to belong. It is also for those who are still learning how to receive love after years of believing they had to earn it.

At the heart of this story is a quilt. Each piece of fabric carries a life, a wound, a memory, a prayer, or a moment of hope. No single piece tells the whole story. Together, however, the pieces become something larger than themselves. The quilt becomes a picture of community, connection, healing, and the possibility that what has been broken can still become beautiful.

The pages that follow are fictional, but the emotions behind them are real. The characters carry questions that many people have asked in quiet places: Can I still be loved? Can I belong? Can faith and identity exist without fear? Can the wounds left by rejection actually heal?

My hope is that these pages meet you gently. I hope they remind you that your story matters, that your life has value, and that you do not have to hide your pain in order to be worthy of compassion. Most of all, I hope you encounter a love that does not turn away when life becomes complicated.

There is room for healing. There is room for grace. There is room for your story.
