Preface

Love That Covers began with a conviction that love can meet people in the places where life has left them wounded. The first edition explored that conviction through a story of hurt, forgiveness, faith, and restoration. It asked what can happen when people choose compassion instead of allowing pain to have the final word.

This LGBTQIA+ edition grows from that same foundation, while telling a distinct story. It is not a sequel or a replacement for the first book. Its focus turns toward identity, rejection, faith, and the longing to belong without having to hide the truth of one's life. The connection between the two editions is the belief that every person deserves to be treated with dignity and compassion.

At the center of this story is a quilt, first brought together through the hands of people who each carry a different history. The pieces are not identical, and neither are the lives represented by them. Some carry memories of kindness; others hold the weight of rejection, loneliness, or words that were difficult to forget. As the quilt moves from one person to another, it becomes a reminder that no single life tells the whole story and that care can connect people who might otherwise feel alone.

The chapters follow that connection as it grows. We meet people through the pieces they carry, consider what remains after love has been given or withheld, and see how a simple thread can bind separate lives together. The story also asks what it means to receive love, to offer it without conditions, and to keep choosing compassion when the journey becomes difficult.

These characters and events are fictional, but the emotions are familiar to many people. The questions beneath the story are real: Can I be honest about who I am and still be met with kindness? Is there room for me when I have been made to feel unwelcome? Can trust grow again after rejection? Can a community become a place of care rather than another place to fear?

This book is for readers who have carried those questions themselves, for those who love someone who has, and for anyone willing to listen with an open heart. It does not ask you to know every answer before you begin. It asks you to make room for another person's story and to remember that compassion is not diminished when it is shared.

My hope is that these pages offer a thoughtful place to pause, recognize the humanity in one another, and consider the lasting difference that love can make. May the quilt remind you that every piece matters, every story deserves to be heard, and no one should have to earn the dignity of being treated with care.

There is room for healing. There is room for connection. There is room for your story.
